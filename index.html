What we do

Complete solutions.
One seamless experience.

Choose a single service or combine several. Every contract comes with one point of contact and clear reporting.

01

Asset management

We look after your buildings, equipment and other assets so they stay safe, usable and comfortable. Regular upkeep now saves you time and money later.

02

Concierge

A welcoming first point of contact for guests and visitors, on hand around the clock to help with whatever they need. Well suited to hotels, serviced apartments and commercial lobbies.

03

Mechanical and electrical systems

When power, heating, cooling or other plant fails, operations stall and staff and customers notice. Our qualified contractors keep your systems serviced and running reliably.

04

Fire and compliance

Fire safety equipment only protects people if it works when needed. We schedule regular checks and servicing, and keep your site compliant and ready for an emergency.

05

Energy management

We review your energy and utility bills, find where you are using more than you need, and help you make changes that lower costs and your environmental footprint.

06

Security

Trained security professionals protect your building or property and keep restricted areas secure.

07

Maintenance

Our multi-skilled tradespeople and handymen cover repairs inside and out, including electrical, plumbing, carpentry, gardening, odd jobs and general fix-ups.

08

Landscaping

Well-kept grounds give your property a professional first impression. Our gardeners handle mowing, planting and general grounds care, using lower-emission, quieter equipment to limit disruption.

09

Space planning

Moving to a bigger site or reworking your current one? We help you lay out the space so it is safe, practical and comfortable for your team and your customers.

10

Cleaning

Janitorial and cleaning services for offices and facilities, from weekly or fortnightly routines to occasional deep cleans, delivered by trained cleaners to a consistently high standard.

11

Housekeeping

We manage housekeeping for properties of every size, from small family-owned apartments and hotels to larger chains, so guests enjoy their stay and you have one less thing to manage.

12

Stewarding

Experienced kitchen stewards keep commercial kitchens and cooking equipment clean, orderly and safe, so you can focus on the food.

13

Event planning and catering

End-to-end planning for businesses large and small, including venues, decor, catering, music and DJs. Suited to exhibitions, reunions, anniversaries, end-of-year parties and private events.

14

Stationery and office supplies

We track stock and reorder for you, so your office never runs short of stationery and supplies.

15

Waste management

Collection, treatment and disposal of all waste types in any volume, plus recycling and resource recovery where possible.

16

Pest control

Qualified technicians deal with insects, rodents, weeds and other pests. We identify the risks to your site and bring them under control before they become problems.
