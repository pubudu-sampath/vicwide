VICWIDE FACILITY MANAGEMENT - WEBSITE PACKAGE

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a web browser.
3. Keep the assets folder next to index.html.

FILES
index.html: Complete website with embedded CSS and JavaScript, including 3-slide hero carousel.
assets/hero-slide-1.jpg: Your building. Our responsibility.
assets/hero-slide-2.jpg: Clean spaces. Better experiences.
assets/hero-slide-3.jpg: Well-managed spaces. Lasting impressions.

NOTES
- Google Fonts are loaded from the internet (DM Sans and Manrope). System fonts are available as fallback.
- The enquiry form currently opens a mail client using placeholder info@example.com; replace this address with your business email before deployment.
- Company contact placeholders also need updating before public deployment.
- The slideshow photos are design visuals, not photographs of actual VICWIDE-managed properties.
- No backend server is included or required for preview; to receive enquiries automatically, configure an appropriate form backend.
